Sequences (sim settings)
========================
A sequence is a list of pages shown one after another (page flip). Page fields:
  text     template: {name} {icons} {zone} {zone_name} {destination} {dest_icons} {ext_dest_icons}
           {next_stop} {next_icons} {ext_next_icons} {via} {via_stop} {via_icons} {ext_via_icons} {line} {time} {message}
  font     font id (e.g. 0 thin, 3 normal) – optional
  spacing  letter spacing – optional
  seconds  how long the page stays (default: settings "seconds"); running texts get at least
           the time to scroll through once
  when     always | request (stop on request) | via (route has "via") | zone (stop has a zone)
           | not_request
  role     text | heading – on two-line panels a heading is shown on the upper line together
           with the following page on the lower line

Sequences: next (next stop), destination (at a stop / line + destination), exterior (front/side/rear),
zone, time, message, final, stop (stop name at a stop).
